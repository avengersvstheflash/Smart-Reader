About Smart Reader

Smart Reader is evolving into a personal intelligent digital library for novels, webtoons, manga, textbooks, research materials, and notes.

Architecture & Build 1 Features

  • Modular Architecture: Clear separation between UI, API, Business Services, Repositories, and AI Providers.
  • AI Provider Abstraction: Independent of Ollama implementation details, ready for future Gemini models.
  • Separation of Concerns: AI summaries are stored as generated representations and never mutate original user content.
  • Local-First Persistence: SQLite structured storage for zero-dollar, private local execution.

Settings & AI Configuration

How Smart Reader AI Works

Smart Reader is engineered with a Private Local AI architecture. When active, it connects to your local Ollama daemon (llama3) to generate chapter breakdowns, summaries, and reading intelligence.

Your books, chapters, and research notes stay on your machine and are never transmitted to external third-party cloud services.

🛡️
Full Offline Guarantee: All core library management, chapter reading, structured canonical formatting, typography preferences, and search features function completely without AI.
