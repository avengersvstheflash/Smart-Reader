About Smart Reader

Smart Reader is a personal digital library with chapter summaries and reading intelligence for novels, manga, textbooks, research materials, and notes.

Architecture & Guarantees

  • Immutable Originals: Original content is strictly preserved and never mutated by AI operations.
  • Persistent Representations: AI summaries exist as distinct, persistent representations rendered in the main reading canvas.
  • Structured Canonical View: Markdown is normalized into structured blocks so raw syntax never leaks into the reader.
  • Flexible AI Processing: Full support for both Local AI (private offline execution) and Cloud AI.
